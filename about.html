The Tribe

A gym built on people. Strength, skill and community — in that order of importance, depending on the day.

Our story

More than a gym

My Tribe Gym was created for people who want more than machines and mirrors. We wanted a place where you walk in and feel at home — where coaches care about how you move, and members care about how you’re doing.

Under one roof we bring together functional fitness, calisthenics, weightlifting, injury prevention, nutrition and private coaching, led by a team of specialists who each bring their own discipline to the floor.

Whatever brings you in — getting fit, getting strong, coming back from injury or just finding your people — you’ll find your tribe here.

What we stand for
